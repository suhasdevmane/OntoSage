## How a target is read

A target is a REDUCTION against a stated baseline by a stated date, set by a named
authority. Progress against one is a calculation over metered consumption and the
baseline — never a claim this register makes on its own, and never a figure to quote
without saying which baseline it is measured from.

Status is the owner's assessment, recorded here and not re-derived from consumption.
