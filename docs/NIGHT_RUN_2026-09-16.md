## The headline

Three answers that looked like routing bugs were **missing data**. The building had no record
of what a door does when power fails, and no water temperatures at all, so two questions had
nowhere correct to land and a third had no vocabulary to be understood by. Adding the records
and the points turned them into correct answers; only then did the routing guards matter.

## Defects found and fixed (all with tests)

* **BUG-609 (P1)** — water temperatures were judged against the **air** band (−30..70 °C), so a
  normal 72 °C heating flow was reported as "physically impossible". A sensor's band now comes
  from its most specific declaring class, never from whichever match the store returned first.
* **BUG-610** — a per-sensor read scanned the 10.1M-row table instead of using its key.
  **20.21 s → 2.33 s** for the same 10,440 rows; warmest floor **86 s → 36 s**; the whole probe
  28.3 → 20 min.
* **BUG-606** — narration presented one property as evidence about another and contradicted
  itself. A duty is not a schedule; a runtime is not a setpoint; the item called highest must be
  the highest figure listed.
* **BUG-611** — a plant-circuit reading was answered from the patrol register ("circuit" is one
  of its lay terms). One pattern now guards all three paths that resolve a record class.
* **BUG-613** — a field filled on every record was read as "nobody lacks it", while eight
  records said "No cover, next working day".
* **BUG-614** — after the route finder stopped claiming route comparisons, they fell into the
  compare lane's canned template. A comparison of recorded times is a question about records.
* **CAVEAT-604** — a question word that is also a recorded status now yields both readings.
* **BUG-616** — "longest unseen" was read off the interval column instead of being computed.
  Where a register records a last-something date and an interval, the system now computes the
  elapsed days and how far past the interval each record is.
