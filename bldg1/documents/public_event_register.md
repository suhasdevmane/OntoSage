# Public Event Register — Abacws Building

_**Synthetic demonstration record** — fictional events, not a real programme._

## What a visitor needs to know

A visitor's questions are not a room booking's questions. A booking says a room is held; it
does not say when the doors open, which entrance to use, or whether a companion needs a
ticket of their own. This register holds the published arrangement, and it is deliberately
allowed to disagree with the booking behind it — **that disagreement is the answer to "has
the venue changed since the information I received"**.

**A moved event keeps its previous venue on the row.** An absent `previous_venue` means the
register records no move, which is not the same as a guarantee that nothing changed; an
answer should say which of the two it is.
