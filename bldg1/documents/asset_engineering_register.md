# Asset Engineering Profile Register - Abacws Building

_**Synthetic demonstration record** - fictional commissioning figures, not a real O&M file._

## What this register records, and what the building recorded before it

The building already recorded that an air handling unit **exists**. It never recorded what it
was **for**. That is the whole gap this register fills, and it is why mechanical and
electrical engineers, insurers and facilities managers were the three worst non-occupant
roles after eleven other registers had landed.

Three design decisions carry the register:

- **Three duty columns, not one.** `design_duty` is the claim on the drawing,
  `commissioned_duty` is what was actually witnessed and signed off, `observed_duty` is what
  is measured now. Collapsing them would make the only interesting question - *where do they
  disagree?* - unaskable. AHU Floor 2 was commissioned 6% below design and signed off at that
  figure; AHU Floor 3 is delivering 78% of what it was commissioned for. Those are different
  problems with different owners, and a single "duty" field could state neither.
- **Three tag columns, not one identifier.** *"Are asset names consistent across drawings,
  BMS and maintenance records?"* is only answerable if the inconsistency can be represented.
  A single canonical identifier would assert the consistency the question is asking about.
- **A blank `observed_duty` is a finding, not a gap.** AHU Floor 5 has no flow measurement
  fitted, so its performance is **unknown** - which is exactly what an engineer must be told,
  rather than an estimate or silence.

`isolation_proven_on` is separate from `isolation_point` for the same reason: a documented
isolation point nobody has proved dead is a claim, and two assets here have never had one
proved at all.
