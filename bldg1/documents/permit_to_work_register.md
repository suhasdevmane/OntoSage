# Permit to Work Register — Abacws Building

_**Synthetic demonstration record** — fictional history, not a real compliance document._

## When a permit is required

A permit to work is required for: **hot works**, **roof access**, **confined space
entry**, **work on or above ceilings where asbestos is registered**, **electrical
isolation above 230 V**, and **work at height requiring anchor points**.

Hot works are controlled by permit **without exception**. A hot work permit requires a
fire watch during the work and for 60 minutes after completion.

## Roof access

Roof access requires a permit issued by the Estates Compliance Team on the day of the
work. A **harness permit** is required in addition wherever work takes place outside the
guarded walkway. Anchor points must be within certification (see the fall-arrest section).

## Issuing and closing

Permits are issued by the Estates Compliance Team and countersigned by the contractor's
authorised person. A permit is **not closed by its expiry date** — it is closed when the
issuer signs it off. A permit past its expiry that is still open is an exception and is
escalated to the Compliance Manager at the next working-day review.

## Contractor competency

No permit is issued to a contractor without a current site induction and, for the permit
types above, the relevant competency on file. See the contractor control record.
