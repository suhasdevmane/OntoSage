# Fire Safety Procedures — Abacws Building, Cardiff University

## Emergency Contacts

- **University Security (24/7)**: 029 2087 4444
- **Emergency Services**: 999
- **Fire Safety Officer**: fire.safety@cardiff.ac.uk

## Evacuation Procedure

1. On hearing the fire alarm (continuous bell), **leave the building immediately** via the nearest fire exit.
2. Do NOT use the lift/elevator during a fire evacuation — use the stairs only.
3. Assembly point: **Maindy Road car park** (north side of Abacws building, 50 metres from main entrance).
4. Report to your floor warden at the assembly point.
5. Do NOT re-enter the building until the fire brigade or building manager gives the all-clear.

## Fire Exit Locations (Abacws)

- **Ground floor**: Front main entrance (south), rear service exit (north), stairwell A (east wing)
- **Floors 1–4**: Stairwell A (east wing), Stairwell B (west wing) — fire doors propped open only via approved hold-open devices
- **Floor 5 (Computer Science)**: Stairwell A (east), emergency door to external walkway (south elevation)
- **Roof terrace**: Do NOT use as an assembly point; evacuate to ground level

## Personal Emergency Evacuation Plans (PEEPs)

Occupants with mobility impairments must register a PEEP with the Estates team. Designated refuge areas are located at each stairwell landing. Contact estates@cardiff.ac.uk to arrange.

## Fire Detection System

- Abacws has an **addressable fire detection system** covering all floors
- Detectors: optical smoke detectors, heat detectors, manual call points (red boxes near exits)
- The detection system is monitored 24/7 by University Security
- **OntoSage does not interface with the fire detection system** — fire alarms activate building hardware independently of the digital building intelligence platform

## Fire Warden Contacts

Each floor has designated fire wardens (updated termly):
- **Floor 5**: Contact the School of Computer Science and Informatics reception
- **Floors 1–4**: Contact School administration offices on each floor
- **Ground floor**: Security desk at main entrance

## Annual Fire Drills

Full building evacuations are conducted once per academic year (typically October). Partial tests are conducted in alternate semesters. Results and evacuation times are reviewed by the Fire Safety Officer.

## Prohibited Actions

- Propping fire doors open with non-approved devices
- Storing combustible materials in corridors or stairwells
- Disabling or covering smoke detectors
- Blocking fire exit routes with equipment or furniture
