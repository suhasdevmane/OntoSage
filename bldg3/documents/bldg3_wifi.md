# BuildSys Building (bldg3) — WiFi & Network Access Policy

Guests connect to the **BuildSys3-Guest** wireless network; no password is required and a
captive portal accepts the terms of use. Staff and students use **BuildSys3-Secure** with
their building credentials (eduroam is also available across all four floors).

For connectivity problems, contact the IT service desk at ithelp@buildsys.example or
call extension 4000. Coverage is provided on every floor including the atrium and the
IoT Systems Lab.
