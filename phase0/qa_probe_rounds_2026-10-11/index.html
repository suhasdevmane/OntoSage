    QA probe rounds, 11 October 2026 — stakeholder intent coverage¶

    Plan: tasks/PLAN_2026-10-11_STAKEHOLDER_INTENT_COVERAGE.md. All asks went through scripts/qa_probe.py (Open WebUI's headers, /v1/chat/completions, streamed, all four caches flushed per ask) as the QA account facility03@example.com. Nothing here is a study form, study room or study wording. Bank questions are counted, never printed.

    PASS in the probe means "routed and delivered", not "correct". Every answer below was read by hand; where the probe's decline detector and the read disagree, the read is what is reported.

Live: yesterday 3,449 L; week on week 66,882 L against 66,494 L.
    • Utility cost (TODO-1646): metered total × the registered tariff in force, standing charge per day, "rates exclude VAT", no figure when no single tariff covers the period; tariff rate units transcribed from the tariff document as triples. Live: water last week ≈ £130.09; electricity yesterday ≈ £699.79.
    • Kitchens (TODO-1634): the four kitchen/break rooms located as amenities, repeating the catering topic's own statement about microwaves and filtered water (floors 2–4).
    • Exports (BUG-1644): CSV / PDF / Word written in the format named, LINKED in the reply, rows carrying sensor name and unit, documents carrying a per-sensor table, the period and (kWh/L) the total. Floor plans (BUG-1645): the render shown inline, the PDF linked, no IRI.

    Not authored, on purpose (NOTE-1635): lockers, vending machines, a UPS, work-order due dates and a maintenance response-time policy — the building's own text states none of them, and a location or policy invented for a real building is a plausible wrong answer.

    Named weekdays and the study (CAVEAT-1616)¶

    The weekday resolver was deferred on 10 October because 25 of the 56 study texts name a weekday. Re-measured 11 October: those 25 fields all carry a full date with a year, which the explicit-date path resolves first, so 0 study fields are newly bounded. Residual: a participant who types "on Wednesday" WITHOUT the task's date now gets the most recent Wednesday, where before they got the default recent window — neither is the task's date, so it is not worse, but the study key should expect the date to be stated.

    M1 re-asked on the final build (same 208 questions, same account)¶

    Declined 50/208 before and 50/208 after — the day's fixes did not move this sample's decline count. 15 moved declined → answered and 15 answered → declined; median 17.5 s (was 15.2 s). Reading the 15 new "declines" (answers only): about nine were declines in the baseline too, in a wording the detector did not count ("I did not find any answer in the records I searched", "I couldn't map part of your request"); two replaced a weak answer with an honest decline (advice invented from noise sensors; a ranking recommending the main reception); one is now a clarification ("Which space should I check?"); two are answers the detector misread; and one is a routing change caused by classifier variance (recommend instead of deliberate, then event_store_query moved it to the events lane — CAVEAT-1677). Read this as: the detector's decline count is too noisy to show a change of this size; it is not evidence of improvement or of regression. The fixes were aimed at the conversations and the stakeholder pools, where each one was verified by re-asking.
