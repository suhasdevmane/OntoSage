    How do glare sensors work? Does that come from the monitor itself? — run 1 (9.5 s, OK)¶

    Glare sensors detect unwanted reflected light that reduces a screen’s legibility. They typically use a photodiode or phototransistor mounted near the display to measure the intensity of light reflected from the screen’s surface. The sensor’s output is compared to a preset threshold; if the reflected light exceeds that level, the system can dim the backlight or adjust the display’s brightness. The sensor does **not** read the monitor’s own emitted light; it only senses ambient or reflected light that falls on the screen, so it is an external component rather than part of the monitor’s internal circuitry.
