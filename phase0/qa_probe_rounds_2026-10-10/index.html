    QA probe rounds through the Open WebUI endpoint — 10 October 2026¶

    Scope. bldg1 (Abacws), live stack, questions asked through /v1/chat/completions exactly as Open WebUI asks them (its chat-id and user-email headers, streamed), as the QA account facility03@example.com, with all four answer and routing caches flushed before every ask. scripts/qa_probe.py records the route (ontosage_route), the answer, and whether every chart image it links actually loads.

    What this is not. It is not an evaluation. Every question here was written by the developer, and every defect found was fixed against the question that found it, so these questions are tuned-on. "PASS" from the probe means routed to an expected lane and delivered without an error, an unexpected decline or a missing image; it does not mean correct. Correctness was judged by reading the answers, and several PASSes were wrong (they are in the tables below). No Paper 3 study task, room, date or criterion appears in any probe question.

    What was wrong, and what changed (tracker BUG-1503 … BUG-1561)¶

    Wrong room, wrong quantity, wrong scope (P1s). - A room id was dropped when the hinted sensor class was narrow: "temperature in room 1.06" read the outdoor weather feed; "light levels in room 4.03" read six floor-level sensors (BUG-1503). - "Is room 5.01 warmer than room 3.10?" lost room 3.10 and said it had no temperature data (BUG-1514). Each named place is now resolved. - "What maintenance was done last month?" listed six static issue CATEGORIES as "all the maintenance actions recorded" (BUG-1520). It now reads the work-order register by date. - Forecasts started where the training span ended, two days in the past (BUG-1538), and a building "total" forecast was the mean of single sensors, ~30 people for ~225 (BUG-1539). - "Will floor 3 be warmer than floor 4 tomorrow?" was asserted from two past means 0.1 °C apart (BUG-1545). Each floor is now forecast and the forecasts compared with their bands (services/forecast_comparison.py). - Floor water flow was labelled L/s and integrated to 12,661 m³ in a day (BUG-1557). - "Which staff member badged in first today?" was not refused (BUG-1551).

    Routing (each shape measured over the 4,060-question bank before it was added). Sensors that stopped reporting → freshness lane; incident and near-miss logs, work orders, who maintains / services / cleans → the registers; "what does the building measure" and "what sensors are in room X" → the reach answer; imperative comfort requests ("make room X warmer") → control; "what's on in room X tomorrow" → bookings; a room id like "1.06" no longer splits a question as a numbered list; the post stage no longer undoes a register decision.

    Registers. A named period ("in September", "last month", "next year") now filters on the date that says when a record happened, ended or was completed; a floor named against free-text locations ("Level 4") filters by storey; "When does X expire?" gives the end date; a counted "none" is no longer rewritten as a search miss.

    Charts. Light-level charts were refused as the wrong quantity; bar and forecast charts never reached Open WebUI (the executor's output key was misread, the forecast code imported a module the sandbox forbids, and base64 charts are not rendered by Open WebUI); bar charts now start at zero and name floors.

    Narration. Chat shorthand ("temp in rm 5.01 now") is expanded; "muggy" is a humidity word, not a CO2 one; comparatives ("warmer", "noisier") name their quantity; "the day before" is a baseline; a count followed by a quantity word is no longer flagged as an impossible reading; QUDT units print as units.

    Round 3 (tracker BUG-1563 … BUG-1582)¶

    Round 3's 60 "PASS"es hid at least 14 wrong or empty answers; every one below was found by reading the answer, and all but two are verified live after the fix.

    • A per-day or per-hour question got one figure for the whole period (BUG-1563). "The daily average temperature in room 4.18 for the last 7 days" now returns one row per day and "CO2 in room 1.06 hour by hour yesterday" one row per hour, reduced in the store (no row cap), days aligned to the building's midnight (services/aggregate_breakdown.py).
    • A carbon-monoxide question was answered with the floor energy meters (BUG-1567, P1): the word "carbon" inside "carbon monoxide" also resolved the carbon-footprint concept. A concept whose only evidence sits inside another concept's longer term is now dropped (47 of 4,060 bank questions change, all a generic concept losing to a specific one).
    • "List the open work orders for the lifts" was refused as a control command (BUG-1568, P1); a verb after a determiner ("the open") is no longer an order.
    • Energy from power readings was summed, not integrated (BUG-1582, P1): "solar energy yesterday" said 135 kWh; it is now about 34 kWh, stated as an integral of the kW readings.
    • A limit the reader states is used (BUG-1572): "did any room go over 1000 ppm yesterday?" → "Yes … on 104 of 280 sensors (the limit you gave)".
    • Named rooms by kind ("lab 4.20", "computer lab 1.07") are places (BUG-1565, BUG-1569); a booking request is an availability check that says the booking itself is not made here (BUG-1569); "how bright is the seminar room 3.13" is a reading, not the public-event register (BUG-1566, two shortcuts); a floor-named total is checked against placement instead of vetoed (BUG-1571); "how many CO2 sensors" counts CO2 sensors (BUG-1573); "too humid" gets a verdict against the listed standards (BUG-1574); chart captions are in building time (BUG-1570); a point's own QUDT unit is no longer dropped (BUG-1581); the route record names the contract rules that fired (CAVEAT-1526); "which sensors stopped reporting" reaches the freshness lane.

    Data (TTL-first, synthetic, no study referent): the chiller's water points are typed chilled water supply/return and the vocabulary knows those words (BUG-1564); a rooftop-solar topic and a gas-leak procedure were authored through the admin Capabilities API (BUG-1575/1576); a PV_Generation_Power_Sensor class, the PV points typed with it and declared in kW, a solar generation shape in the publisher (zero at night), and the last week of PV rows regenerated with it, backup in outputs/backups/ (BUG-1580).

    Deliberately not changed, because they would move answers for study rooms days before the freeze: linking floor 0–4 rooms to their HVAC zone (CAVEAT-1577), cleaning the floor-4 plan's ground-floor labels (CAVEAT-1578), extending the term timetable past 18 September (CAVEAT-1583), and typing the boilers' water points.

    Round 4 (tracker BUG-1584 … BUG-1593)¶

    Round 3's fixes generalised: every breakdown phrasing ("for each day this week", "hour by hour today", "daily … over the last 5 days" on a floor), every stated limit ("above 26 degrees", "more than 60% humidity"), every room named by kind ("office 4.07", "classroom 1.09"), and the new plant and solar vocabulary answered first time. Reading the answers found ten more defects, all fixed and verified live:

    • "Which contracts expire before the end of the year?" said two dates existed, both passed (BUG-1585, P1): the verb "expire" selected the state "expired". Six contracts end this year, three of them still to come; the answer now lists all six with their status.
    • "How many PM2.5 sensors?" answered "one" (BUG-1586, P1): "2.5" in "PM2.5" was read as a room.
    • An indoor question no longer counts the outdoor sensor (BUG-1584); a change question ("gone up since this morning") is answered by comparing the last hour with this morning (BUG-1587/1593); "will it get stuffy this afternoon?" is forecast and answered against the standards band — "Unlikely: peaks at 865 ppm, the band's edge at 988, inside the 1000 ppm limit" (BUG-1592); a comparison answers its yes/no first (BUG-1588); "electricity use per floor" is a total, drawn as a bar chart (BUG-1591); "energy rating" reads the building's recorded rating, not its meters (BUG-1590); "when was the building opened?" reads its build year (BUG-1589).

    Open from round 4: a forecast's "measured skill" line reported an MAE of ~800 ppm for one room (CAVEAT-1594): the skill registry was graded on 28 August, before the generator fixes, and should be re-graded after the study freeze.

    Round 5 — conversations (tracker BUG-1599 … BUG-1614)¶

    Single questions were by now mostly right, so round 5 asked 12 multi-turn conversations (32 turns, docs/phase0/conversations_r5/, scripts/run_conversations.py, one chat id per conversation, as facility03): a value then "and the X?", a ranking then "there", a count then "which floor has the most of them?", a register list then "the first one" and "it", a judgement then "what about room Y?", a solar total then "and the day before?". In the first run 10 of the 20 follow-up turns and 3 of the 12 opening questions had a defect, and the worst were confidently wrong rather than declined. Each was traced in the log to its cause and fixed, then the conversations were asked again after a restart with all four caches flushed (outputs/qa_probe/conversations_r5{,b,c}.jsonl).

    The two findings worth carrying forward.

    1. The relevance gate's protection of grounded answers (BUG-873) never covered the aggregate lane. _grounded_evidence_behind looks for figures on the bus copy of aggregate_result, and the SQL node never copied them, so any store-computed answer the gate objected to was replaced by "I couldn't answer that from the records". Found only because a follow-up gave the gate the bare "and the day before?" to judge. Both halves are fixed (BUG-1608).
    2. The v2 compound router took a question the events lane answers well and sent it to a lane that cannot check the period it names. A kind of room plus a free window, and nothing else, now stays with the events lane (4 of 4,060 bank questions; 0 of the 56 study texts mention availability). A third facet (quiet, seats, a projector) still escalates. Separately, a deliberation ranking in which every space scores 0 is no longer presented as a recommendation (BUG-1607).

    New single questions after the fixes (scripts/qa_probe_questions_r5.yaml, 9 questions): 9/9 delivered, read by hand: per-floor counts of CO2, noise (ontosage:Sound_Level_Sensor, found through the lay-term resolver because no Brick class name says "noise") and occupancy sensors; "free tomorrow morning" and "available this evening" from the events lane; "who is assigned to WO-010?"; and "which QUIET meeting rooms are free tomorrow afternoon?" now names availability under "Not checked" (BUG-1615) instead of implying it was checked. One is still weak: "How much energy did the solar panels produce on Wednesday?" reads ten days and says no kWh exists, because a bare weekday is resolved by nothing deterministic (CAVEAT-1616) — and that is deferred for the study freeze: 25 of the 56 study texts name a weekday, so changing weekday resolution now would move study answers.

    Every new rule was measured on the 4,060-question bank before it was kept; most move 0 bank questions because conversation phrasings ("the two", "its", "who is assigned", "what time does it close") are rare in single questions. Still open: the deliberation lane cannot check availability for a named period (CAVEAT-1617; it now says so); a two-subject amenity question ("which floors have accessible toilets and where is baby changing?") declines under the capability subject test (CAVEAT-1618, the live test test_floor_word_does_not_steal_capability fails on it; not caused by this round); a follow-up that only changes the place ("what about room 2.53?") drops the "too humid?" judgement (CAVEAT-1619); and a clarification the user ignores ("and the CO2?" after "which one do you mean?") is answered for the room the classifier picked from the previous reply, which the answer now names but does not flag as assumed.

    Round 6 — a second, NEW conversation battery (tracker BUG-1620 … BUG-1626)¶

    Ten new conversations (docs/phase0/conversations_r6/, 30 turns) written after round 5's fixes, to see whether they generalise to new follow-up shapes. Most did: a possessive ("what's its leaving water temperature?" → the chiller only, then "and the boiler's?"), a per-floor count of water meters, "compare it with room 3.16" then "which is quieter?", "and the day before?" on floor humidity, the nearest toilet after the nearest drinking water. Seven new defects, all fixed and re-asked live; one was a regression from round 5's own fix:

    • "What's the temperature in office 4.07?" → "I can't find 'office 4.07'" (BUG-1624, P1): the v2 compound router read "in office" as choosing an office, and the deliberation lane could not match "office 4.07". A kind plus a dotted id is now one named place (0 of the 451 bank questions that select spaces change). Now 22.3 °C.
    • The rewrite invented a year (BUG-1620, P1): "plot it" became "… on Saturday, 10 October 2023", and since written years are authoritative the chart found nothing. A year that no message contains is now removed, and the rewrite prompt keeps "today" as "today".
    • Record ids were cut at their first number (BUG-1626, P1, my regression from BUG-1603): "the second one" of a contract list became "CON-2025", which two contracts share. Ids now keep every part, and Unicode hyphens are normalised. Now "CON-2025-003 — Clearview Facilities … ends 4 November 2026".
    • "how much was that?" after "Floor 4 used the most electricity" is answered (BUG-1625); "when was it highest?" keeps the CO2 instead of becoming "when was floor 2 highest?" (BUG-1623), and a single day's peak is that day's hours, not "usually around 14:00" (BUG-1622); a per-hour breakdown is now plotted as a line chart (BUG-1621; verified: two charts linked and loading).

    Open from round 6 (minor, no study shape): "is it working?" after the nearest drinking water reads the room's sensors, not the water point's status (CAVEAT-1627); "what did they record yesterday?" after a count of water meters declines (CAVEAT-1628). A follow-up that turns "the day before" into "Thursday, 8 October" (no year) cannot be resolved to a window — the deferred weekday and date family (CAVEAT-1616).
