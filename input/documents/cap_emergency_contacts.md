# Abacws Building — Emergency Contacts

Emergency contacts for Abacws: (1) Emergency services: 999; (2) Cardiff University Security (24/7): 029 2087 4444 or internal x74444; (3) First aid: trained first aiders are on each floor (names posted on floor notice boards), or call Security; (4) Estates emergency helpdesk (out-of-hours building issues): 029 2087 5555; (5) Health & Safety: safety@cardiff.ac.uk. First aid kits are located at each floor reception point and in labs. AED (defibrillator) units are on the Ground Floor and Floor 3.
