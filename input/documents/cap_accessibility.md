# Abacws Building — Accessibility

The building is fully accessible: (1) Step-free access at the main entrance and all secondary entrances; (2) Passenger lifts on the main atrium serve all floors; (3) Accessible toilets on every floor; (4) Hearing induction loops at reception and main lecture spaces; (5) Accessible parking bays on Senghennydd Road (blue badge required); (6) Refuge areas at each stairwell landing for evacuation assistance. For disability-related queries, contact the Disability and Dyslexia Service (disability@cardiff.ac.uk).
