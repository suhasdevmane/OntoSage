# Abacws Building — Smoking Policy

The Abacws Building is a no-smoking and no-vaping building, in line with Cardiff University's smoke-free policy. Smoking and the use of e-cigarettes / vapes are not permitted anywhere inside the building or at the entrances. Designated smoking areas are located away from building entrances outdoors; please use those and dispose of cigarette ends in the bins provided.
