# Abacws Building — Transport Parking

Getting to Abacws (Senghennydd Road, Cardiff CF24 4AG): (1) By train: Cardiff Central station is ~15 min walk or 5 min taxi; (2) By bus: Routes 8, 9, 26, 27 stop on Cathays Terrace (2 min walk). Frequent service from Cardiff city centre; (3) By bike: secure bike storage in the basement (swipe card access); on-street cycle parking on Senghennydd Road; (4) By car: limited on-street pay-and-display parking nearby; Cardiff University has a car park on Corbett Road (permit required for staff). Electric vehicle charging points are available in the car park area. Disabled parking bays (blue badge) are on Senghennydd Road directly outside the entrance.
