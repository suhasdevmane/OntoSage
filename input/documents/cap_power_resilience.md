# Abacws Building — Power Resilience

The building has an uninterruptible power supply (UPS) for critical systems: emergency lighting, fire alarm panels, server rooms, and security systems. A diesel standby generator provides emergency power for server rooms and critical lab equipment within ~30 seconds of a mains failure. General lighting, heating, and non-critical office equipment will lose power in an outage. The building can maintain basic safety functions (alarms, emergency lighting, security) during a power outage but normal operations (teaching, lab work) will be suspended.
