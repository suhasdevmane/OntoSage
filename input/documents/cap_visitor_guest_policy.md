# Abacws Building — Visitor Guest Policy

Visitor policy for Abacws: (1) All external visitors must sign in at the main reception (Ground Floor) and will be issued a visitor badge; (2) Visitors must be escorted by a Cardiff University member of staff or student at all times; (3) Visitor wifi: use 'CU_Guest' network (self-registration at the portal); (4) For large groups or events, notify Building Management in advance; (5) Contractors and suppliers must check in with Security and complete a building induction. Visitors are not permitted in server rooms, labs, or other restricted areas unless specifically authorised by the area owner.
