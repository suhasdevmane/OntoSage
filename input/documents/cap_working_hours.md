# Abacws Building — Working Hours

Standard building hours: Monday–Friday 07:00–22:00; Saturday–Sunday 08:00–18:00. The building may open earlier or close later during exam periods. Out-of-hours access (after standard hours) is available to authorised staff and registered postgraduate students with appropriate card access. The building is generally closed on bank holidays except for security-monitored access. Check the Cardiff University website for holiday opening schedules.
