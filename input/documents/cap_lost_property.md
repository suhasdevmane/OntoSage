# Abacws Building — Lost Property

Lost property for the Abacws Building is handled at the ground-floor reception desk. Hand in or report any found or lost items there during staffed hours. For items lost across Cardiff University more widely, contact the central Security & Estates helpdesk. If reception is unattended, email estates at estates@cardiff.ac.uk with a description, date, and location.
