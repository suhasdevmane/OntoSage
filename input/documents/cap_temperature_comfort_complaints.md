# Abacws Building — Temperature Comfort Complaints

If you find the temperature uncomfortable in Abacws: (1) The building HVAC is zone-controlled — occupants cannot directly adjust thermostats; (2) Report comfort complaints to the Facilities helpdesk (estates@cardiff.ac.uk or extension 76026) describing the specific zone or room number, floor, and whether it is too hot or too cold; (3) Facility management can investigate and adjust zone setpoints; (4) Sensor data is available via OntoSage to check whether the measured temperature is within the ASHRAE comfort range (20–24°C). Persistent issues should be escalated to the Building Manager.
