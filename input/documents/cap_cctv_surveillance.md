# Abacws Building — Cctv Surveillance

CCTV cameras are installed throughout the building's public areas, corridors, entrances, and car parks. Cameras operate 24/7 and footage is retained for 30 days. The system is monitored by Cardiff University Security Services. CCTV signage is displayed at all camera locations in compliance with GDPR requirements. For CCTV requests (e.g. incident investigation), contact Security Services or submit a Subject Access Request through the university's data protection office.
