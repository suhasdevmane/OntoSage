# Abacws Building — Lighting

The building uses LED lighting throughout with daylight harvesting and occupancy-based presence detection. Lights automatically adjust to maintain target lux levels and switch off in unoccupied areas. Ceiling-mounted PIR presence sensors control individual zones. External shading (automated blinds) on south-facing facades reduces solar gain. Manual override switches are available in some office areas. Lighting faults should be reported to facilities management.
