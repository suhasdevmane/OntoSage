# Abacws Building — Printing Scanning

Printing and scanning is available via Multi-Function Devices (MFDs) on every floor. The system uses Cardiff University's PaperCut print management. To print: (1) Send a document to the 'Secure Print' queue from any university computer; (2) Swipe your university card at any MFD to release your print job. Colour and A3 printing available on all floors. Scanning is free — scan to email or USB. Print credit can be topped up online at print.cardiff.ac.uk. For issues contact the IT Service Desk (itservicedesk.cardiff.ac.uk, 029 2251 1111).
